This is OFFLINE synthetic infrastructure, NOT scored research, not a
new preregistration, and not permission to execute orders.

- **Date blocks:** 10,000 moving-block replicates resample complete
  same-day instrument clusters; seed and block length are explicit.
- **Placebo:** 10,000 random entry schedules preserve instrument,
  whole-share count, holding duration, one-at-a-time exposure, and
  adverse 30-bps-per-side costs. **Stop-first exit dynamics, matching
  notional risk and true capital capacity remain UNVERIFIED.** Such
  placebos cannot yet satisfy the promotion gate.
- **Deflated Sharpe:** a per-period nominal probability penalizes
  at least the 80 historically inspected trials from the ledger and
  any supplied additional tests. Serial correlation and true
  effective independent trial count are UNVERIFIED, so this nominal
  number is never marked promotion-valid.
- **Benchmark:** a SPY total-return-index proxy applies owner-supplied
  actual deposits after the next market close when necessary,
  identically to active NAV. Reports time-weighted return and
  annualized money-weighted XIRR. Passive units are **fractional
  research index units**, not whole-share executable SPY; passive
  slippage is modeled at 30 bps on each purchase/liquidation mark.
  Active equity must already include fees and the exact same deposits.
